The Game

An open-world highway combat racing sim set on the roads of Gauteng, South Africa. Death Race weaponized rolling death machines meets Driv3r difficulty scaling, Burnout Revenge chaos engineering, and real sim-grade physics.

Free-Roam

Drive an unmodified vehicle through a seamless, infinite rolling horizon. No weapons, no armor — pure survival. Fuel burns constantly, forcing real engagement decisions.

Combat Events

Race against AI opponents that learn how you play via neural networks. Earn scrap, fuel and ammo. Every event is different — road conditions degrade in real time.

Workshop

Your garage is your safe zone. Rebuild, armor up, switch vehicles and plan your next run. Manage SCRAP / FUEL / AMMO economy to survive the highway.

Real Terrain

Built from actual SRTM satellite heightmaps of Gauteng — 44,373 km² of real South African terrain at 30m resolution. No hand-sculpted roads.

Features

  • Infinite Rolling Horizon — The highway never ends. World tiles load seamlessly around you.
  • Real Road Degradation — Potholes, cracks and erosion evolve as you drive. Roads get worse.
  • AI Rivals with ANN — Opponents use artificial neural networks that adapt to your driving style.
  • Economy of Scrap & Fuel — Earn parts in events, spend them in the Workshop. Fail and lose your vehicle — keep salvage.
  • South African Setting — East Rand / Gauteng. Real geography, real landmarks, real elevation data.
  • Unreal Engine 5 — C++ gameplay code. World Partition for streaming. Nanite-ready destruction.

Development

Solo dev. Real-time updates. No gatekeeping.
