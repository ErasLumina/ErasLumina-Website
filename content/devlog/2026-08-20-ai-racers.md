A racing game with dumb AI is a treadmill. A racing game with AI that **studies you** is an opponent. That's the design goal for PotHole Dodgers' rival racers, and it's the most ambitious system in the project.

## The idea

Every race you run gets recorded. Those recordings feed neural networks running inside Unreal Engine, so the AI doesn't just drive a fixed line — it adapts to how *you* play. Aggressive? It learns your racing lines and starts blocking them. Cautious? It finds where you hesitate and punishes it. The highway never ends, and neither does what the AI knows about you.

## Where it stands

- **Research is active.** The reference library covers neural networks for game AI, RL-in-Unreal theses, and GameAI Pro material — the theoretical ground is solid.
- **The learning pipeline needs migration.** The LearningAgents work from earlier experiments still needs to be ported into the current UE 5.8 project structure. That's the next concrete step on this front.
- **Physics first, brains second.** The AI is only as good as the car it drives — so vehicle physics (the J160 gearbox spec) lands before the neural layer goes live on top of it.

## Why it matters for the loop

This is what makes "every race teaches the AI something new" more than a tagline. It turns progression into a two-way street: you upgrade your car, and the world upgrades its defense against you. Destruction is progression — but so is being studied.
